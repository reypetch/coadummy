The COA NFP Group

Four Pillars of the
COA Cooperative

The COA NFP Group was established to bridge the gap between regional land-sharing and professional governance, technical stewardship, and community housing.

COA Land Lots
Management Operator
Professional Management Operator of high-density residential zones using the Land Lease Living framework. 6-month maximum site deployment and secure long-term residency.
Read More →
COA Land Care
Parent NFP Regulator · S68 Holder
The official Section 68 (S68) Holder providing Governance Shield, technical steering, and WHS Technical Sanctioning across the 10,000-hectare corridor.
Read More →
COA Housing Charity
Registered CHP · Crisis Outreach
Registered Community Housing Provider managing Crisis Outreach Accommodation. Transitions residents from housing distress into permanent stability via a "Housing First" model.
Read More →
COA Land Trust
Institutional Asset Vault · BSA Holder
Institutional Asset Vault and primary Biodiversity Stewardship Agreement holder. Zero-debt entity designed to vault high-value land assets in perpetuity for the community.
